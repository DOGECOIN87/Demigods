# QA — Twelve procedural mouths (2026-07-27)

`mouths` held one asset. DG-198, recovered from the base master, was the only
mouth in the collection, so every one of the 777 tokens would have worn the same
expression. The asset catalogue rendered the category as a single cell, which is
what made it obvious.

Registered as DG-237 to DG-248. The category goes from 1 to 13.

- Sheet: `composites/mouths_2026-07-27.png`

## Build

`scripts/build_mouths.py`, signed distance fields at 4× supersampling inside a
152 × 86 band on the locked anchor (627, 441). Nothing is drawn outside it.

Closed mouths are tapered strokes along a quadratic Bezier — a radius that
shrinks from the centre of the stroke to its ends, which is what gives a painted
mouth line its lift at the corners. Open mouths are filled ellipses whose outline
band is taken from the same distance field rather than stroked separately, so the
outline width stays even all the way round; inside sits a darker interior, a
tongue across the lower two-thirds, and for one design a fang.

Ink is `(136, 65, 33)`, sampled from the darkest opaque pixels of DG-198, so the
procedural mouths and the recovered painted one read as the same hand rather than
as two different line weights on one face.

## Uniqueness and diversity, measured

Three checks, because each catches a different kind of sameness.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Exact duplicates (SHA-256 of pixel data) | **0** of 13 |
| Shape overlap, max IoU of ink masks | 0.668 (`tiny_round` vs `pink_open_pout`) |
| Colour distance over the ink union, min / median / max | **75.4** / 100.1 / 124.2 |

### The first set was not diverse, and eyeballing it did not show that

Five of the twelve closed mouths — `short_line`, `flat_line`, `tiny_neutral`,
`tiny_curve`, `soft_curve` — differed only in **length**. Measured at thumbnail
size, `short_line` and `tiny_neutral` sat **0.94** apart on a scale whose maximum
across the set was 17.35. They were the same mouth five times, and on the
contact sheet they looked like a reasonable range of small mouths.

The closed set now varies by shape instead: curvature sign, asymmetry, waviness,
and whether the line is broken. A smirk, an omega cat mouth, a wavy line and a
parted line cannot be confused with each other or with the neutral.

### Then the same problem appeared in the open mouths

With the closed set fixed, shape overlap showed the open mouths were the
near-duplicates: every one was an ellipse at the same anchor, running 0.62–0.70
IoU against its neighbours. They now differ by silhouette — two flat-topped
D-shaped grins at 32 and 60 px wide, a round "o", a tall pout, and a wide snarl
with fangs — rather than by size alone.

### Two floors that are not defects

`tiny_round` and `pink_open_pout` still measure 0.668 IoU, because two small
openings centred on the same anchor necessarily overlap. They differ in aspect
and in colour: a dark void against a pink-filled oval, 92 apart on the colour
metric.

`flat_line` and `wavy_unsure` are the closest pair at 75.4. At 210 px a closed
mouth is roughly one pixel tall, so **no** closed mouth is distinguishable from
another at thumbnail size. That is a property of the scale, not of the designs;
what reads at thumbnail is open versus closed.

## Corrections worth keeping

The downturned mouth was first drawn as a 3 px arc, indistinguishable from
`flat_line` at face scale. It now drops 7 px, matching the smile's rise. An arc
that *measures* as curved is not necessarily one that *reads* as curved.

The fangs were first positioned relative to the unclipped ellipse top. On a
flat-topped design the clip left them one pixel of room and they never appeared
at all. They now hang from the shape's actual upper lip.

## Known limits

**These are shapes built to the sheet's descriptions, not the sheet's paintings.**
The twelve `FACE` mouth cells are distinct artwork with their own lip structure
and colour; DG-095 to DG-106 stay pending and are not satisfied by these.

Numbered from 014 so 001–012 stay free for the sheet cells and 013 stays with the
recovered mouth.

The open mouths carry no lip-corner shadow and no upper-lip line, so at full
resolution they are simpler than DG-198. At 210 px the difference does not read.
