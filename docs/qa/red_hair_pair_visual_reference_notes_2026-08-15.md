# Red Hair Pair — Visual Reference Notes

## Source-sheet observation

The dedicated hair catalog is a 128 × 96 pixel reference and establishes the eighth upper-row/lower-row pairing as the red family. It is suitable to identify the intended family, color, and distinct silhouette but is not production art. The eighth rear-hair cell reads as a deep red/auburn long wavy back layer, while the eighth front-hair cell reads as red short bangs with short face-framing pieces.

## Approved collection anchor

The approved teal rear-hair candidate was inspected as a style and rig reference only. It establishes the collection’s isolated transparent-layer standard: premium anime-chibi rendering, crisp dark teal/navy linework, controlled cel and painterly highlight bands from the upper-left, a transparent open face cavity, centered crown at the shared scalp, and sufficient hidden overlap around the head/shoulder region. It is not a silhouette template for the distinct red design.

## Red pair acceptance criteria

The red rear asset must be materially distinct from the teal family, retain a transparent face opening, remain centered on the shared scalp and behind the head/shoulders, and carry clean long wavy red/auburn locks. The front asset must contribute only the short bangs and face-framing strands, preserve an unobstructed view of the eyes and face, and match the rear family in hue, linework, lighting, scale, and overlap.
