# Rear Aura Batch DG-154, DG-155, DG-157 — Visual Review Notes

## Review scope

The pale-blue ice-crystal, black-smoke void, and cyan water-splash floor rings passed binary, alpha, canvas, floor-aura rig, and provenance-aware intake validation. They were reviewed over the neutral base and in full context with the registered red matched-hair pair and navy high-collar coat against the bright celestial throne hall and dark violet void portal.

## Findings and decision

**Approved for registration.** Each variant retains the approved wide elliptical ground-plane role and transparent central foot opening. The ice ring reads through facet-value shifts rather than a flat pale fill, satisfying the earlier design rejection that ruled out purely procedural ice shards. The smoke ring remains shallow and visibly dimensional without becoming an opaque ground shadow, while the water ring has compact low splash peaks that do not collide with the lower outfit silhouette.

All three are centered under the character with their lower visible edge seated at the locked Y 1139 foot baseline, as required by the reduction-only normalizer’s trait-safe canvas constraint. The full-context composites show no face obstruction, clipping, background matte, scene contamination, or visually dominant collision on either reviewed background. The effects are distinct by material and rendering behavior rather than color alone.
