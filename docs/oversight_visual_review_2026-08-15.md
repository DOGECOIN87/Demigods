# Oversight Visual Review — 2026-08-15

## Scope

This review compared the refined Athena trait-sheet reference with the existing cross-category stress composite. It is an art-direction checkpoint, not an approval of any new asset.

## Findings

The collection’s intended visual language is a polished chibi-fantasy character system: front-facing presentation, soft rounded anatomy, large brown eyes, pale skin treatment, long stylized hair, ornate celestial/arcane environments, luminous effects, and high-contrast blue, violet, gold, and warm cream palettes. The subject must remain the primary visual anchor in every composition.

The current stress composite establishes that the registered base body, hair-back layer, selected background treatments, and floor-ring auras can share a consistent canvas and readable depth hierarchy. The composited subject remains centered and visible across the reviewed backgrounds. No dominant collision, clipping, or obvious misalignment is visible at the review scale.

The review also confirms the most material creative risk: the available stress composite still depicts the neutral base in an undergarment rather than a fully realized character. This is expected at the current production stage, but it means visual confidence in the eventual collection depends on completing compatible front hair, outfits, face traits, accessories, and hand objects before final character-level approval.

## Direction for Subsequent Batches

Every remaining transparent trait should preserve the approved soft chibi linework, frontal perspective, centered proportions, upper-left key light, and the collection’s celestial/arcane visual vocabulary. Batch review sheets must include composites over at least two contrasting registered backgrounds and, where relevant, one aura or global-finish condition. Any candidate that reads as an independent full character, changes the base-body silhouette, or buries the face must be rejected or re-rendered.

## Legendary and Modular Tier Check

The reviewed Solar Court legendary is a high-fidelity, fully composed chibi-fantasy illustration with a centered hero, strong solar-gold hierarchy, confident celestial architecture, readable facial focal point, and carefully integrated foreground and background effects. It supports the intended 1-of-1 premium tier and is visually distinct from the modular set without departing from its fantasy language.

The five reviewed pose-aware modular outfits retain compatible line weight, warm skin rendering, facial proportion, and front-facing chibi silhouette. They establish useful thematic diversity across celestial, storm, verdant, lunar, and sun-temple directions. However, their head and facial areas remain intentionally incomplete; character-level cohesion cannot be signed off until the hair and face systems are registered and stress-tested with the outfits. This confirms that hair-plus-face completion is the decisive next creative milestone, rather than adding more background or effect variants first.
