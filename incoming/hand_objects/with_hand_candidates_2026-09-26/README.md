# Hand-and-object candidates — 2026-09-26

Six generated replacement candidates, uploaded at the owner’s request. Each PNG contains the object and gripping/supporting hand together on a transparent 1254 × 1254 canvas.

**Status: not production-ready.** Image generation did not consistently preserve the base hand position or scale. These sources have not been registered in the production asset manifest and do not replace the current `assets/hand_objects/` files.

## Verified

- Six distinct PNG files, all fully decodable, 1254 × 1254, RGBA.
- Alpha contains both transparent and opaque pixels.
- Original generated bytes retained without resizing, trimming or recoloring.
- SHA-256 and intended base reference recorded in `manifest.json`.

## Outcome (2026-09-27)

All six were fitted; see `docs/qa/hand_objects_in_hand_2026-09-27.md`. The dark wand (003) and star spellbook (005) were registered from byte-identical copies in `images/trait_candidates/hand_objects/in_hand_2026-09-27/`. The silver sword (004) and gold lantern (006) need new renders: the sword is drawn too long for its grip, and the lantern keeps a painted wrist stub. The arcane staff (001) and violet orb (002) were not used; the round-1 versions of both were fitted again instead.

## Required before registration

> **Update 2026-09-27:** the owner dropped the wrist-removal step. A candidate with a painted wrist (here, the gold lantern) is generated again without one instead of being trimmed. See `prompts/hand_objects_handover_2026-09-27.md`.

Fit each hand to its specified base pose. Remove any wrist extension, especially the lantern’s wrist stub. Match the palm-to-arm attachment and cover or mask the base hand so there are no extra fingers. Inspect a composite over the corresponding base and dressed-body poses. Transparency and dimensions alone do not establish overlay compatibility.
