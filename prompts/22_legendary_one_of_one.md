# Prompt — Legendary 1-of-1 Demigods (7 pieces)

Seven unique pieces, one per domain. Unlike the 770 generative tokens these are
**complete flattened compositions**, not modular layers: character and
environment are painted together in a single full-bleed image.

That difference is the whole point. A modular token can only ever be a legal
stack of approved layers, so it can never have a two-handed gesture, an
asymmetric silhouette, contact between character and scene, or an effect that
wraps in front of and behind the body at once. A 1-of-1 can. Each brief below
names the specific thing the layer system cannot express — that element is what
earns the piece its rarity, so do not drop it.

What must *not* change is family resemblance. Same camera, same chibi
proportions, same rig, same lighting. A legendary should read instantly as the
same collection, rendered further.

## Shared output contract

Paste this block with exactly one piece brief appended. Attach
`assets/base_bodies/base_body_001_neutral_master.png` and

```text
Create exactly one Demigods LEGENDARY 1-of-1 illustration: a single complete
character-and-environment composition, rendered natively at exactly 1254 x 1254
pixels as a PNG in sRGB.

ATTACH: assets/base_bodies/base_body_001_neutral_master.png (proportion and
lighting reference only) + docs/rig/rig_guide_1254.png

CANVAS AND OUTPUT:
- exactly 1254 x 1254, generated natively at that size
- never upscale, downscale, resample, or outpaint to reach 1254 x 1254
- RGB or fully opaque RGBA; fill every canvas pixel, no transparent border
- one finished illustration only; no contact sheet, variants, or side panels

FAMILY RESEMBLANCE (this is what makes it the same collection):
- one front-facing chibi character, orthographic, zero yaw/pitch/roll/tilt
- match the attached master's head-to-body ratio and head size exactly
- head centred near X 627 with the crown near Y 141 and the eye line near Y 367
- feet resting on the ground plane at Y 1139; keep the figure within X 233-1021
- soft upper-left key light ~45 degrees, lower-right form shadows, subtle cool
  right rim, soft neutral ambient fill
- controlled cel shading with refined painterly rendering, crisp anti-aliased
  edges, coherent line weight, premium anime-fantasy game art
- the face keeps the collection's expression language: large clear eyes, small
  nose, simple mouth; no realistic anatomy, no aged or adult proportions

WHAT A LEGENDARY MAY DO THAT A MODULAR TOKEN MAY NOT:
- character and environment painted as one image, with real contact between
  them: cast shadow on the floor, light spill onto the ground, cloth or hair
  overlapping scenery
- asymmetric pose, two-handed gesture, and held or worn objects that would break
  the shared hand anchors
- effects that pass both behind and in front of the body in the same image
- bespoke costume construction that does not have to survive layer swapping

QUALITY BAR:
- readable at thumbnail size: silhouette first, then one focal accent, then
  detail; do not fill every square inch
- richer rendering than a standard token, not busier composition
- values must stay separated - the character must not sink into the background

PROHIBITED:
- text, letters, words, numbers, runes, pseudo-writing, signatures, watermarks,
  captions, UI, frames, borders, or presentation layout
- photorealism, three-quarter or side views, camera tilt, perspective
  distortion, lens effects, depth-of-field blur across the whole frame
- extra or missing fingers, duplicated limbs, malformed hands
- more than one character, creature companion, or floating disembodied head
- named real-world characters, franchise likenesses, or copied designs
- gore, weaponry aimed at the viewer, or anything outside an all-ages fantasy
  register

Return one PNG only, with no explanatory text.
```

## Production notes

**These bypass the generator.** `scripts/generate_777.py` composes modular
layers; a flattened 1-of-1 has no layers to compose. Render them, QA them, and
place them into the output set by hand at fixed token IDs.

**Decide the supply arithmetic before minting.** Two options, and they are not
interchangeable once the collection is public:

* **770 generative + 7 legendary = 777.** Keeps the headline number. Set
  `supply` to 770 in `config/collection.json` and reserve seven token IDs.
* **777 generative + 7 legendary = 784.** Leaves the generative run untouched
  but changes the collection size.

The first is the usual choice. Either way the saturation maths barely moves —
the rule-valid space is 3547, so dropping to 770 changes nothing material.

**Gating differs from every other asset.** A legendary is full-bleed and
opaque, so `--trait`, `--floor-aura` and `--global-finish` all fail it by
design. Check instead: native 1254 x 1254, no transparent pixels, character
within X 233-1021 and Y 129-1139, feet on Y 1139, and no text anywhere in frame.

**Metadata.** Give each piece a `Legendary` trait value and a `1 of 1` edition
marker rather than per-category traits, since it has no layer breakdown. Record
each in `assets/asset_manifest.json` with `origin: native_image_generation` and
its own QA note.

**Do not apply the global finish or the background depth pass.** Both exist to
make separately-produced modular parts agree with each other. A 1-of-1 is
painted as one image and is already internally consistent; treating it a second
time flattens contrast it was composed with.
